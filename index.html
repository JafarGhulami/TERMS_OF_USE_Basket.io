Basket

A shopping list app by GITI Tech

Privacy Policy Terms of Use

Basket helps you write your grocery list fast. Start typing and it suggests common US grocery items in English or Spanish, with type, size and brand if you want them.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas: jghulami2000@gmail.com. We reply as soon as we can.

Your data

Your lists stay on your phone. Basket has no account and does not send what you type to any server. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Data sources

Item names, sizes and common brands come from U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Research Service. FoodData Central. fdc.nal.usda.gov. USDA does not endorse Basket.
