The short version

This version of Basket collects nothing about you. Your lists are stored only on your phone. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app makes no network requests.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your shopping lists and the items, quantities and choices (type, size, brand) you add.
  • Items you type that are not in the built-in catalog, so they can be suggested again.
  • Your settings, such as language and light or dark appearance.
  • A built-in catalog of common grocery items that ships with the app.

This information never leaves your phone. We cannot see it. Suggestions are worked out on your phone; what you type is not sent anywhere.

Backups

Basket turns off Android's automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer for its data, so your lists are not copied to Google's backup service. This also means a new phone does not get your lists automatically.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings > Apps > Basket > Storage. Either one permanently deletes everything Basket stored. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Permissions

Basket does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Children

Basket is meant for adults planning their shopping. It is not directed to children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not collect any.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information. Since Basket keeps your data only on your phone, you can see, change and delete all of it in the app or by uninstalling it. For any question, write to us at the address above.

Future features

Later versions may add features that need the internet, such as reading receipts, price estimates, ads or subscriptions. Before any version sends data off your phone, we will update this policy, show what changes, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new date. Material changes will also be shown in the app before they take effect.
